# Bobbinly Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 10, 2026

Bobbinly is a Chrome extension that copies an email thread from Gmail so you can paste it into an AI tool of your choice. This policy explains what Bobbinly accesses and what it does with it.

## The short version

Everything happens on your computer. Bobbinly has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking. Your emails are never sent to us or to anyone else.

## What Bobbinly accesses

**Email content.** When you click "Copy thread" (or press Alt + B), Bobbinly reads the email thread you have open in Gmail: senders, dates, message text, and attachment file names. It formats this text and copies it to your clipboard. It does not store the content, and it does not send it anywhere. Bobbinly only reads a thread when you ask it to.

**Your account email address.** Bobbinly reads your Gmail address from the browser tab title, only to check whether the latest message in a thread was sent by you. It is not stored or sent anywhere.

**Your Spool (optional).** If you fill in "Your Spool" (your name, a short description of yourself, and how you write), Bobbinly saves it using Chrome's built-in storage so it can be added to your prompts. If Chrome Sync is turned on, Chrome syncs this information across your own devices through your Google account. Bobbinly's developer never receives it. You can edit or clear it at any time in Your Spool.

## What Bobbinly does not do

- It does not send your data to any server, including ours.
- It does not use analytics, ads, or tracking.
- It does not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone.
- It does not use your data for anything other than copying the thread you asked for.

## Permissions

- **Access to mail.google.com:** to read the open email thread when you click "Copy thread."
- **Storage:** to save Your Spool.

## Chrome Web Store Limited Use

The use of information received from Gmail by Bobbinly adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
